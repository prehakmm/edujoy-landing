Frequently asked questions

How long is a lesson?

Every lesson is 45 minutes long (an academic hour). A course with 4 lessons a day means 4 × 45 minutes of teaching.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, classic online and in-person courses can be paid in 3 instalments at no extra cost.

When do the courses take place?

On weekdays in the morning and evening; weekend courses are available too. We adapt course times to our students' schedules.

Where are the in-person lessons held?

At the EduJoy school, Purkyňova 74/2, Prague 1, next to Národní třída metro station.

What's the difference between an intensive and a classic course?

Intensive courses have 60–150 lessons and run several times a week or daily. Classic courses have 48 lessons – twice a week, 2 lessons at a time.

I don't know which level to choose.

No problem. Call us on +420 604 181 400 or fill in the form and we'll help you choose.
